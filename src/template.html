Implementation
NYC Reads phases
Districts adopted the NYC Reads curricula in waves. Because the waves started in different years, the 2026 results carry different amounts of exposure. This page lines those groups up.
Read this before using this page. Districts were not assigned to phases at random, and the groups did not start from the same place. Differences below are descriptive contrasts between groups of districts, not estimates of the effect of NYC Reads. Nothing here isolates the programme from everything else that changed in those districts over the same years.
Who started when
Launch timeline as published by NYCPS. District 75 appears in several phases but is not reported in the district file, so it cannot be shown here.
Exposure at the time of each test
The elementary curriculum covers K–5, so it reaches tested grades 3–5. The middle-school rollout reaches grades 6–8. A test taken in spring 2026 sits at the end of school year 2025–26.

Comparability
Paper and computer administration
NYSED moved the ELA test onto computers grade by grade. Because the switch happened in different years for different grades, the grades themselves fall into three groups, and those groups can be compared.
What this can and cannot tell you. Grades differ from one another for many reasons that have nothing to do with how the test was delivered, and each group here is only two grades. This page shows that the timing of the switch lines up with a movement in the results; it does not establish that the format caused it.
